SkillSwap - Register page
=========================
Files:
  register.html      -> the register/sign-up page
  css/register.css   -> all styles (colors/font are in the :root variables at the top)
  js/register.js     -> show/hide password + form validation

How to use:
  1. Unzip the folder.
  2. Open register.html in your browser (or copy the files into your project).
  3. The 'Log in' link points to login.html - keep both pages in the same folder.
  4. Search for 'TODO' in js/register.js to connect your backend / Google sign-up.
